Verified 2026 Portal Status & Enlistment Monitoring

Welcome to Nigeria Recruitment Tracker, an independent educational portal and official portal monitor dedicated to assisting aspiring Nigerian candidates through statutory military, paramilitary, and civil service enlistment exercises.

Our research team continuously monitors public gazettes, agency bulletins, and official recruitment portals including the Nigerian Army Regular Recruit Intake (88 RRI), Nigerian Navy Basic Training Course (Batch 39), Nigerian Air Force (BMTC 45), Nigeria Police Force (NPF Constable Enlistment), Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC), Nigeria Customs Service (NCS), Nigeria Immigration Service (NIS), and the Federal Civil Service Commission (FCSC).

Official Anti-Fraud Advisory & Free Application Notice

All official Nigerian Armed Forces and federal paramilitary application forms are strictly 100% free of charge. Never pay individuals, social media agents, or unauthorized third parties for application forms, screening passes, or enlistment slots. Report fraudulent solicitations immediately.
